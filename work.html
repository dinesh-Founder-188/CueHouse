    Destinations
    It’s a special part of the world where the honking of tuk-tuks and the rush of waterfalls create a perfect symphony. Welcome to our home; we can’t wait to share our favourite places with you.

    Most people have heard of the Taj Mahal. But most people also couldn’t tell you where to savour fragrant, pillowy pittu in Jaffna. As your guides, that’s a responsibility we take very seriously. Look over there, just past the crowds: there is magic.

    Every one of our trips is a story of a thousand threads, reflecting the vastness and variety of our wonderland. Ancient monasteries meet the hottest local haunts, and tea-covered hills spill into dreamy island getaways. Together, they form a unique and unforgettable story; one that only you could tell.

    Destinations

    Sri Lanka

    Rise to the call of temple drums. Ask Mendis to show you his favourite local surf spots. End the day feet-in-sand, or tucking into the best Godamba roti that Google can’t find. Where will Sri Lanka take you?

    Let's go to Sri Lanka
    “On the morning train to Ella. Mist-covered valleys and the brightest green hills stretch out in every direction. But the real action is happening inside.

    At one end of our carriage, someone’s beating a drum. At the other, a vendor selling pol pani wafts the sweet smell of coconut my way. I had a huge breakfast at the hotel, but I wouldn’t say no to one of those pancakes right now. I can’t wait to see the tea plantations up close but for now, I’m just enjoying the ride.”

    Destinations

    India

    Heart-thumping skydive over Aamby Valley, or skin-tingling massage at Taj Lake Palace? Whether you’re after culture, nature, lounging or adventure, there’s (so much) more to explore with Red Dot. Where will India take you?
    Let's go to India
    “Road-tripping through rural Rajasthan today. Made all the more magical by red-turbaned goat herders and flashes of beautiful saris in the dusty air.

    Arriving in Udaipur is quite the contrast in the best way possible. The honking of tuk-tuks in traffic, roadside chai, a million windows... I can’t wait to get out there to explore the palaces and stepwells with our guide later. Right after I’m done admiring the insane views of Lake Pichola and the Aravalli mountain range from our terrace.”

    Destinations

    Maldives

    Come face to fin with the deep-sea locals. Set adrift on a paddleboard. Or ground yourself for a yoga session on the beach. Where will The Maldives take you?
    Let's go to Maldives
    “As we prepare our diving gear, I feel like a kid setting off for Disneyland. Only I'm about to meet a real-life Nemo.“
    We reach a quiet lagoon, the water so clear we can spot an eagle ray gliding next to our boat. One (ungraceful) backflip into the sea later, and I've slipped into another world. A coral reef during rush hour. Clownfish, parrotfish, moray eels, a blacktip shark, even a hawksbill turtle! Back at our overwater bungalow, I'm already daydreaming about tomorrow's snorkel and sundowners.”

    On our radar

    Venture into Ladakh

    It’s easy to get lured into more mainstream sides of India but we urge you to look beyond that. Ladakh, in India’s north, offers another world, where monasteries are perched on mountain tops and snowy peaks contrast with blue skies. Here you’ll find total peace, truly remote and authentic villages, trekking routes, and turquoise lakes. Recent improvements to accommodation make this now a very accessible and comfortable region, which is perfect between May and September.
